Reach audit, on the post itself

A browser extension adds one line under a post. Opening it shows where the post's reposts and likes came from.

What 38% means
Of the post's 8,516 reposts and likes, 3,236 came from accounts the model scores 0.5 or higher. The story-wide baseline is 4.1%.
What it never says
Who runs an account, or whether the post is true. Private accounts are counted, never named.
Where it leads
"Open full check" shows the posts, timestamps and reasons behind every number in the panel.
