How to report this

  • Say “likely automated accounts”, not “bots”. These accounts have not labeled themselves.
  • Cite the interval: 18% (95% interval 13 to 24%), at the default score cutoff of 0.5.
  • Do not attribute. We do not know who runs these accounts, and this is no ruling on whether the claim is true.
