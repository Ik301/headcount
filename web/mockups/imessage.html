Text it a claim. It texts back who is pushing it.

The text line, in three screens: a judge scans the card on the table, texts a claim they heard, then asks for the evidence behind the numbers. No app, no login; the answer is a few lines, a chart and a link.

Mockup with sample data.

What you can text the line

  • any claim or storyNarratives, likely automated share with a 95% interval, what those accounts share, who amplifies them, a chart and a link.
  • show the botsThe strongest coordination evidence: identical text or links posted within seconds, and when the accounts were created.
  • who shares itThe archetypes of real accounts amplifying likely automated ones, with lift over matched similar accounts.
  • nextThe same breakdown for the next narrative.

What it never does: rule on whether a claim is true, say who runs an account, or name a private account. Undisclosed accounts are “likely automated”, with a score and reasons, never “bots”.
