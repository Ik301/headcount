## Scope
Web app (`web/app`): claim checker landing + claim result (Operate), guided story (Read), challenge (Operate). Judges at a team table, newsroom editors, T&S analysts, people on phones.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A check reads like election-night returns: each narrative is a race, posts are the count, and "likely automated" is the share you must subtract before calling anything. Refuses the dark-SaaS dashboard with a glowing accent and the cream-broadsheet-serif-with-red-accent page.
OWN-WORLD: Tally-sheet paper (cool grey-green #ECEEE7), green-black ink, one process-magenta reserved for likely automated (not party red or blue), steel blue for real amplifiers; a drenched counting-room green band (#10261F) for the landing. Schibsted Grotesk (a newsroom's own grotesque) for every word and every figure, heavy and tight for counts; Fragment Mono only for receipts, timestamps and ids. Hairline rules, returns-table rows, hatched intervals, verdict stamps.
STORY: The visitor sees the volume, watches it sorted, sees the automated share with its interval and a plain verdict, then opens any number to its receipt.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: green band, question set large at left, the claim field directly under it with Check, three example claims as a numbered list, the real network globe at right. Result: the claim echo, then the headline count and share in heavy figures, then the returns table.
FORM: Returns desk (grounded list position 6 of 7). seed: none (unavailable; seed printed only as raw key). Raises: rows re-rank without breaking columns (split-flap); every claim sits beside its proof (monochrome marketing); on phones one decision per line (airport wayfinding).
Signature motion: "the count comes in": rows report in order, bars draw from zero, the interval hatch lands after the point, the verdict stamps last; reduced motion keeps only short fades.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
